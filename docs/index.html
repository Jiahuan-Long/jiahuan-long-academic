Research:
Human-centered AI, learning analytics, computational methods

I am a researcher interested in how intelligent systems can support learning, research, and decision-making. My work combines empirical study, data analysis, and interface design, with a focus on building AI-assisted workflows that remain transparent, useful, and accountable to people.

More broadly, I care about the relationship between computation and human judgment: how models represent evidence, where automated systems fail, and how careful design can make those limits easier to notice, discuss, and improve.

I am open to research collaboration, academic talks, and project discussions.
